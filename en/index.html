FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you might want to know before sending your brief.

Our process is AI-assisted from end to end. For simple, single-page websites we can design and build everything autonomously, with no large team, no scheduling bottlenecks, and no endless meetings. That lets us move far faster and keep the price fixed, while still hand-crafting every detail.
Just the brief, and the more context the better. Tell us what the site is for, who your audience is, your goals, plus any copy, logo, brand colours and references you like. The richer the brief, the higher the chance we nail it on the first try. And a round of feedback is always included, so we can adjust the design if needed.
The more context you give us up front, the more likely we are to hit the mark first time. Even so, every project includes one round of revisions, so we can fine-tune the design until it feels right.
Yes, absolutely. You receive a clean, ready-to-publish HTML file that's yours to host anywhere. The code is hand-quality: responsive, accessible (WCAG AA), SEO-ready and optimised for Core Web Vitals. No proprietary platform, no lock-in.
To keep things fast and focused, we don't build on templates or page builders (no Webflow, Framer or Squarespace), and we don't do CMS back-offices, dynamic web apps, or ongoing maintenance, unless you add a monthly support plan.
Once your brief is validated, we'll reach out by email to arrange payment. As soon as the payment is confirmed, we start building, and your site is delivered within one business day.
Yes. Extra pages are available as an add-on at €149 per page (up to 3), with each page adding roughly 24h to delivery.
Yes. If you'd like us to keep your site updated and make tweaks over time, you can add our monthly support plan for €49/month.
Yes. Domain and hosting setup is available as an add-on, so you can go from brief to a live site without dealing with the technical setup yourself.
