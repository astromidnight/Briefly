Who's behind it

Who's behind it.

I'm David Cruz, digital designer living in Portugal, with 16 years of experience working on digital products and websites for clients ranging from early-stage startups to established brands.

Briefly started because I kept seeing the same problem: people needed a clean, professional landing page and had no fast, simple, and affordable options that didn't involve huge amounts of time and a whole team just to manage a simple project.

So I built a service that does exactly that.
